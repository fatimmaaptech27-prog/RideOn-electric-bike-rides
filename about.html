About RideOn

RideOn is a ride-booking service that connects passengers with electric bike drivers who take them to their destinations. It gives people a convenient way to travel without owning a bike.

Our goal is to make everyday travel more accessible while giving electric bike drivers an opportunity to earn by providing ride services. RideOn brings both sides together through a simple ride-booking experience.

Why Electric Bikes

Electric bikes are cheap to run and easy to move through busy roads. This helps us keep rides affordable for passengers and gives drivers a low-cost way to work.

What We Care About

  • Convenience: the driver comes to your pickup location.
  • Affordability: rides that fit a normal budget.
  • Safety: a safe drop at your destination.
  • Opportunity: a way for drivers to earn.

How We Help Drivers

RideOn is not only for passengers. Electric bike drivers can earn by taking passengers from their pickup location to their destination.

By bringing passengers and drivers together in one place, RideOn makes it easier for drivers to find rides and for passengers to find a driver.
