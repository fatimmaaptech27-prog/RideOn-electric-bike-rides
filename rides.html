Find Your Ride

RideOn connects passengers with electric bike drivers who take them safely to their destinations. Whether you need a ride for daily travel or to reach a nearby place, RideOn helps you find a convenient way to travel.

Enter your pickup location and destination, and your driver will come to you.

Rides for Every Day

Electric bikes are a good fit for short and medium distances. You can use RideOn for many everyday trips.

  • Going to work or office
  • Reaching school, college or university
  • Visiting a market or shopping area
  • Meeting family or friends nearby

What You Need to Book

Booking is simple. You only need to give us four things, and then your driver can be arranged.

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your pickup location
  • Your destination

Be as clear as you can with your pickup location so your driver can find you easily.

Why Riders Choose RideOn

Many people find it hard to reach a bus stop or wait for a ride. RideOn solves this by sending the driver to you.

Because electric bikes are cheap to run, rides stay affordable. And since the driver takes you all the way to your destination, you reach on time without any stress.
