How RideOn Works

RideOn connects passengers with electric bike drivers. You book a ride, the driver comes to your location, and drops you at your destination. It takes only three simple steps.

  1. Book Your Ride

    Fill in the booking form with your name, phone number, pickup location and destination. This gives us everything needed to arrange your ride.

  2. Driver Comes to You

    RideOn connects you with an electric bike driver. The driver reaches your pickup location, so you do not have to go anywhere to find a ride.

  3. Reach Your Destination

    The driver takes you to your destination and drops you there. The driver earns by providing the ride, and you get an easy, everyday way to travel.

Tips for a Smooth Ride

  • Enter your pickup location clearly so the driver can find you.
  • Write your phone number correctly (11 digits).
  • Be ready at your pickup location when the driver arrives.
  • Tell the driver your destination again to avoid confusion.

For Drivers

RideOn also helps electric bike drivers. A driver can earn by taking passengers from their pickup location to their destination. If you are interested, contact us from the Contact page.
