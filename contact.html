Common Questions

How do I book a ride?

Go to the booking form on the home page and fill in your name, phone number, pickup location and destination.

Do I have to walk to a stop?

No. Your driver comes to the pickup location you enter and takes you to your destination.

Can I become a driver?

RideOn gives electric bike drivers a way to earn by providing rides. Send us an email to ask about it.

Ready to Ride?

If you do not have any questions, you can book your ride right now. Just enter your pickup location and destination and your driver will come to you.
